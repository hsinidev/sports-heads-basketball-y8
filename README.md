Game Overview


Developed by Crazy Aaron, Sports Heads Basketball is a simple yet entertaining game where you control a basketball player to score points against various opponents. The game has two main modes: single-player mode and multiplayer mode.



How to Play


In single-player mode, the objective is to score as many points as possible by shooting hoops in a row without missing. You have 20 seconds to complete each shot.


The game features different opponents with varying difficulty levels. You can choose from various teams and players, each with their own strengths and weaknesses.



Controls


To control your player, you'll need to use the following keys:





Arrow Keys
: Move left or right


Spacebar
: Shoot


Enter/Space
: Pause game settings


Esc
: Quit game




Objective and Tips


The objective is to score as many points as possible within the time limit. Here are some tips for success:




Practice your shooting skills to improve your accuracy.


Use the right shot: choose between two shots, the one that allows you to shoot from a shorter distance or one that gives more points if made from farther away.


Manage your time wisely: aim to complete each shot within 20 seconds.


Experiment with different opponents and teams to find what works best for you.


Focus on building your skills gradually, rather than trying to play perfectly at first.





Why You Should Play It
